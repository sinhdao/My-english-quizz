# Bài kiểm tra đầu vào

Chạy từng phần một. Gửi đề của phần đó, chờ người học trả lời xong rồi mới chấm và sang phần kế. Tổng thời gian khoảng 60-75 phút; cho phép người học chia làm nhiều buổi và lưu kết quả từng phần vào hồ sơ.

Trước khi bắt đầu, hỏi nhanh: đã từng thi IELTS/TOEIC/chứng chỉ nào chưa (điểm bao nhiêu), mỗi ngày học được bao nhiêu phút, và có lỗi nào bản thân biết mình hay sai.

Người học không được tra từ điển hay dùng công cụ dịch trong lúc làm.

---

## Phần 1: Ngữ pháp và từ vựng (15 phút, 20 câu)

Chọn đáp án đúng nhất. Chỉ gửi đề, không gửi đáp án. Đáp án ở cuối phần.

1. By the time we arrived, the film ____ .
   a) already started  b) had already started  c) has already started  d) was already starting
2. If I ____ more time, I would learn another language.
   a) have  b) will have  c) had  d) would have
3. She suggested ____ the meeting until next week.
   a) to postpone  b) postponing  c) postpone  d) to postponing
4. The number of students taking online courses ____ risen sharply.
   a) have  b) has  c) are  d) were
5. ____ the heavy rain, the match went ahead.
   a) Although  b) Despite  c) However  d) Because
6. This is the house ____ roof was damaged in the storm.
   a) who  b) which  c) whose  d) that
7. Not only ____ late, but he also forgot the documents.
   a) he arrived  b) did he arrive  c) he did arrive  d) arrived he
8. The government is under pressure to ____ unemployment.
   a) tackle  b) catch  c) hold  d) raise
9. Her argument was so ____ that nobody could disagree.
   a) convince  b) convincing  c) convinced  d) convincingly
10. Many people are reluctant ____ their habits.
    a) change  b) changing  c) to change  d) for changing
11. The new policy had a significant ____ on small businesses.
    a) effect  b) affect  c) affection  d) effective
12. I'd rather you ____ smoke in here.
    a) don't  b) didn't  c) won't  d) not
13. Which word is closest in meaning to "mitigate"?
    a) worsen  b) reduce the severity of  c) ignore  d) predict
14. Choose the correct sentence.
    a) There is a lot of informations on the website.
    b) There are a lot of information on the website.
    c) There is a lot of information on the website.
    d) There are a lot of informations on the website.
15. "Urban sprawl" most likely refers to
    a) the decline of city centres  b) cities expanding outward into surrounding land  c) a type of public transport  d) a rise in city crime
16. Had he known the risks, he ____ the offer.
    a) would decline  b) will have declined  c) would have declined  d) had declined
17. The data ____ that most users prefer shorter videos.
    a) suggests  b) suggesting  c) suggest to  d) is suggest
18. Choose the best word: Prices have ____ dramatically over the past decade.
    a) fluctuated  b) fluctuating  c) fluctuation  d) fluctuate
19. She is capable ____ handling the project alone.
    a) to  b) of  c) for  d) in
20. "Their approach is largely ineffective" and "Their approach is of little use" are
    a) opposite in meaning  b) similar in meaning  c) unrelated  d) both grammatically wrong

**Đáp án (chỉ dùng để chấm):** 1b 2c 3b 4b 5b 6c 7b 8a 9b 10c 11a 12b 13b 14c 15b 16c 17a 18a 19b 20b

**Quy đổi sơ bộ:** 0-8 đúng: nền tảng còn yếu (khoảng dưới 5.0); 9-12: khoảng 5.0-5.5; 13-16: khoảng 5.5-6.5; 17-20: từ 6.5 trở lên. Đây chỉ là tín hiệu phụ, band cuối cùng dựa vào cả bốn phần.

Sau khi chấm, nhóm các câu sai theo chủ đề (thì, mệnh đề điều kiện, giới từ, từ loại, collocation) và ghi vào hồ sơ.

## Phần 2: Reading (20 phút)

Gửi đoạn văn và câu hỏi. Người học trả lời trong 15 phút.

**Passage: The Return of the Four-Day Week**

Over the past decade, several companies and a handful of governments have experimented with shortening the working week without reducing pay. Supporters argue that employees are often less productive in the final hours of a long week, and that a day off gives workers time to rest, which improves concentration when they return. Early trials in small technology firms reported that output stayed roughly constant while staff reported lower stress and fewer sick days.

Critics, however, point out that the results are less clear for industries that depend on continuous coverage, such as hospitals, retail and public transport. In these sectors, a shorter week for one employee often means hiring additional staff, which raises costs. Some economists also warn that early trial results may be unrepresentative: companies that volunteer for such experiments tend to be those already confident that the change will work.

A third group of researchers suggests that the key issue is not the number of days but how work is organised. In several trials, firms that benefited most were those that first cut unnecessary meetings and reporting. For them, the shorter week was less a revolution than a prompt to remove wasted time. Whether the same approach can succeed in organisations with rigid schedules remains an open question.

**Questions 1-4: TRUE / FALSE / NOT GIVEN**

1. Early trials showed that output fell significantly in small technology firms.
2. Employees in hospitals have benefited most from a shorter working week.
3. Some economists think the companies taking part in trials may not be typical.
4. The four-day week has been made compulsory by several governments.

**Questions 5-7: Complete the sentences (no more than three words from the passage)**

5. In industries needing continuous coverage, a shorter week for one worker often leads to ______ being recruited.
6. The firms that gained the most had first reduced ______ and reporting.
7. According to the third group of researchers, the important factor is how work is ______.

**Question 8: Choose the best title**
a) Why Workers Should Demand More Holidays
b) The Four-Day Week: Promising but Uncertain
c) How Technology Firms Changed Working Hours
d) The Economic Failure of Shorter Work Weeks

**Đáp án (chỉ dùng để chấm):** 1 FALSE; 2 NOT GIVEN; 3 TRUE; 4 NOT GIVEN; 5 additional staff; 6 unnecessary meetings; 7 organised; 8 b

**Quy đổi sơ bộ:** 8/8: khoảng 7.0+; 6-7: khoảng 6.0-6.5; 4-5: khoảng 5.0-5.5; dưới 4: dưới 5.0. Đoạn văn ngắn nên sai số lớn; ghi chú rằng cần một đề Reading đầy đủ 3 passage để xác nhận.

Hỏi thêm: người học mất bao lâu, có chỗ nào bị kẹt từ mới hay không hiểu câu. Điều này quan trọng hơn điểm số khi lập lộ trình.

## Phần 3: Writing Task 2 (40 phút)

Gửi đề, yêu cầu viết ít nhất 250 từ, không dùng công cụ hỗ trợ.

**Đề:** Some people believe that university education should be free for everyone, while others think students should pay for their own studies. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

Sau khi nhận bài: đếm số từ, chấm theo bốn tiêu chí trong `rubrics.md` (Task Response, Coherence & Cohesion, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy), cho từng tiêu chí một band kèm dẫn chứng, rồi tính trung bình. Liệt kê tối đa 5 lỗi ưu tiên và đưa bản viết lại một đoạn thân bài cao hơn khoảng 1 band.

## Phần 4: Speaking (15 phút, bằng văn bản)

Người học gõ câu trả lời như đang nói (hoặc dùng nhập giọng nói trên bàn phím rồi dán lại). Nhắc: viết đúng như mình sẽ nói, không chỉnh sửa cho đẹp.

**Part 1 (trả lời mỗi câu 2-3 câu):**
1. Where do you live, and what do you like about the area?
2. Do you prefer to study in the morning or in the evening? Why?
3. How often do you use your phone for learning?
4. Tell me about a hobby you enjoy.

**Part 2 (nói khoảng 1,5-2 phút, viết 150-200 từ):**
Describe a person who has helped you learn something important.
You should say: who this person is; what they taught you; how they taught you; and explain why it was important to you.

**Part 3 (trả lời mỗi câu 3-5 câu):**
1. Why do some people find learning a language more difficult than others?
2. Do you think technology has changed the way people learn? How?

Chấm theo ba tiêu chí: Fluency & Coherence, Lexical Resource, Grammatical Range & Accuracy. Ghi rõ rằng Pronunciation không đánh giá được bằng văn bản. Với Fluency, đánh giá dựa trên độ dài, mạch lạc, cách mở rộng ý và việc dùng từ nối, kèm lưu ý rằng văn bản không phản ánh được độ ngập ngừng khi nói.
